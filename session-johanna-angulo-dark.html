The model passed, but should we believe it?

Contamination, memorisation, and trustworthy safety evals

Johanna built and led the model-evaluation function at a European AI lab from zero, owning release gating and safety benchmarking across LLMs and quantum-compressed models. She now engineers AI governance into production for regulated enterprises, translating EU AI Act and ISO 42001 obligations into technical controls.

She holds two M.Sc. degrees in Artificial Intelligence and a B.Sc. in Computer Science, and is a PhD candidate building a neuro-symbolic agent for variant prioritisation in rare diseases — along with the contamination-controlled benchmarks needed to trust it.
