About us

Building the career network AI safety needs

Who we are

W4AIS started because too many capable women in AI safety were working in isolation, present in the field but disconnected from each other.

We're building the network that fixes that: a place to find roles, get visibility, find a mentor and meet the people who open doors, whether you're already established in alignment, governance or policy, or still finding your way in.

The field is young, and the people who join now will shape what it becomes.

Core beliefs

The field needs every capable person

AI safety is short of people in operations, governance, policy and leadership, and leaving capable people out is a cost it can't afford.

Entry routes matter

Many people who could do this work never find a way in. A network, a mentor and a concrete first step change that.

The field is still being shaped

Now is a good moment to get in and move up, while the field is still young.
