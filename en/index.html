W4AIS Manifesto

The AI being built today will make decisions about people's lives for decades to come. W4AIS exists because the people designing these systems still don't represent the world they will affect. This isn't a quota problem. It's a problem of quality, of safety, of shared future. We are the space where technical knowledge and lived experience sit at the same table. Here, knowledge is shared, not hoarded. Every background adds value, because every perspective makes the systems better. For the world.
