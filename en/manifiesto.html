#W4AIS

We are living through the most important moment in the history of technology. And we are not exaggerating.

The artificial intelligence systems being built right now will decide how people are hired, how they are diagnosed, how they are monitored and how they are governed. And they will keep doing so for decades.

The problem is that they are being built with very few voices in the room.

Only 22% of global AI talent is female. Fewer than 15% hold executive positions. In AI research specifically, we make up just 12%. In Spain, we are 19.5% of tech specialists.

This is not a corporate diversity problem. It is a technical quality problem.

Car safety systems were designed for men, and women are 47% more likely to suffer serious injury in a crash. If this happens with cars, what can we expect from AI systems that learn about us, evaluate us and listen to us?

Facial recognition systems developed by teams that lack diversity perform worse at identifying women's faces, particularly those from ethnic minorities. This is not malice. It is absence. And absence has real consequences.

Why W4AIS exists

Because we are the ones who most often bear the consequences of badly designed systems. Because we are underrepresented in exactly the spaces where decisions get made. And because when someone who has lived a problem firsthand joins the room, the solutions improve.

It was born because there was a gap nobody was filling. But the door is open.

AI safety is not a women's issue. It is an issue for everyone who will live in a world with powerful AI, which is to say, it concerns everyone.

We need people who research, who legislate, who communicate, who design policy, who ask the uncomfortable questions. People who come from different places, who have lived different things, who see what others do not see.

If you share the mission, you are welcome here. The name tells you where we come from. The community tells you where we are going.

What we stand for

AI safety is not a technical problem that a handful of clever people at a handful of large companies can solve on their own.
"Moving fast" does not excuse failing to think about who gets left out.
Building the future with the perspectives of the people who will live in it the most.

What we are building

A community where curiosity counts for more than your CV. Where you can arrive not knowing what alignment means and leave wanting to build a career around it. Where the people who have been doing this for years share what they know with those who have just arrived.

From Spain. For the world.
