As AI systems become more capable, the need for people who can make them safe, understandable and controllable is growing faster than the field itself; yet for many engineers, researchers and students, it is not obvious where to start or what a career in technical AI safety actually looks like day to day.

This session offers a practical look at how people get into the field, what kinds of roles exist across government, research labs and independent research, and which skills and experiences make the biggest difference when making the transition.

Jess Bergs is an engineering lead at the UK AI Security Institute, where she works on human-in-the-loop research tools that enable scientists to run empirical research experiments at scale. Her independent research focuses on how to design for effective, scalable human oversight. Before joining the British government in 2024, she worked on EU Horizon projects and Explainable AI at BBC R&D.

She mentors on BlueDot Impact's Technical AI Safety Project, Algoverse AI Research and SPAR.
