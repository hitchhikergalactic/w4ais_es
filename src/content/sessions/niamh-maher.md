From theoretical particle physics to slot game mathematics, and from there to technical AI safety: Niamh Maher joins W4AIS to look back on the year she changed careers and share what she has learned along the way.

She will talk about her journey into AI safety, the questions worth asking yourself when considering a career transition, and the funding that can make it possible, such as Coefficient Giving’s career development and transition funding and BlueDot Impact’s career transition grants. She will also introduce evaluation awareness, with a look at her current research project with SPAR, and explain how AI Safety Barcelona supported her transition and how she now contributes as an organiser. The talk will be followed by a Q&A.

Niamh completed her PhD in Theoretical Particle Physics at the University of Edinburgh in 2023 and then moved to Barcelona, where she first joined the effective altruism community. She worked for almost three years as a slot game mathematician before receiving a career transition grant from Coefficient Giving to move into technical AI safety. 2026 can be described as her AI safety year.

This session is for anyone thinking about moving into AI safety from another field, people early in their AI safety career, and anyone curious about what that transition really looks like from the inside. No preparation needed, just bring your questions.
